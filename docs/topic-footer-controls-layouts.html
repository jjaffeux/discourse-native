Especially apparent on small posts: a floating position indicator can sit directly over the last sentence.

The same control feels less disruptive on a long topic, but its placement still makes the content area unpredictable. The final reading line should never need to negotiate with navigation controls.

Yes, I have been thinking of different solutions to this. A small bottom treatment could make the end of the conversation feel intentional.

It should preserve the full width of the reading column, stay visually connected to the topic, and make Reply feel like the obvious next action without turning it into a persistent distraction.

Keeping the position indicator beside the action also removes one more floating element from the post itself.
