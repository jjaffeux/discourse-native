Share topic: three directions

Mockups for the share dialog on desktop and the share sheet on phones, drawn on the app's own surfaces with the Discourse Dark palette. The controls below switch every frame between palettes, between sharing a topic and a post, and into the copied state. The copy buttons in the frames also respond to a click.

What ships now

Rebuilt from the screenshot, with the problems marked.

  1. The link is cut mid-slug, so the topic id, the post number and the ?u= are never visible, and the topic title appears nowhere.
  2. A page-sized heading for a three-action dialog. Most of the dialog is padding.
  3. "Share to another app" reads as a link and starts at a different edge from the field above it.
  4. "Reply as new topic" isn't sharing. It sits alone in the footer, at a third left edge.
  5. Missing next to the web modal: Invite, the site's share links (X, Facebook, email) and the post's date.
A

Preview card

Show what you're sharing, then two equal actions. Everything that isn't sharing moves to a footer. Same dialog on desktop, same sheet on phones.

Fixes

Title, category and the whole link are visible, wrapped onto a second line instead of cut. One left edge throughout. Copy and Share get equal weight, and Invite and Reply as new topic sit in a footer.

Trade-off

Still a modal on desktop, so copying takes two clicks. The card adds height. On a small phone with a post excerpt, the sheet reaches about half the screen.

Build

Keeps showShellSheet(dialogOnDesktop: true). The rework is inside _TopicShareBody and the footer builder. Invite needs the invite editor to accept a topic, as the web's inviteToTopic does.

B

Share targets

Match the web modal: the link with an attached Copy button, the site's share_links as round one-tap targets, then Invite and Reply as new topic as plain rows. On phones the targets become a row like iOS's own share sheet.

Fixes

Reaches parity with the web: share links, Invite, Reply as new topic and the post's date. The link field keeps both ends visible by eliding the slug's middle. Copy is attached to the field, so the two have the same height.

Trade-off

The most surface of the three. The external targets must hide in private contexts (messages, login-required sites, unlisted topics, read-restricted categories), so the row changes shape from topic to topic.

Build

Largest. Parse share_links into SiteConfig, port Sharing.activeSources with its privacy rule, and open each target's URL outside the app. Middle elision needs a custom text layout.

C

Menu, not a dialog

Sharing is mostly "copy the link", so make that one click away. On desktop the share button opens the shell's menu beside it, with no scrim. On phones the same rows sit in the app's menu sheet.

Fixes

Copying takes one click from the topic header, with no focus trap to dismiss. Every row shares one edge and one size. Built from the shell's existing menu and menu sheet, so nothing new to style.

Trade-off

No room for a preview. The link shows only as a one-line hint under Copy. The web's share links fit poorly in a menu, so this direction stays without them.

Build

Smallest. Replace the dialog with the shell's anchored menu on desktop and its menu sheet on phones. The rows map one-to-one to today's actions, plus Invite.

Recommendation

A on both platforms. It fixes every problem marked on today's dialog, keeps the existing dialog-on-desktop and sheet-on-phone plumbing, and has a natural place for Invite.

If copying is nearly all anyone does on desktop, use C's menu there and keep A's sheet on phones. Take B only if parity with the web's share links is a goal in itself.
