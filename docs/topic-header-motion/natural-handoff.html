A steadier home.

The topic title stays in the top bar from the start. Category editing, category browsing, tag links and the tag editor stay together in one row beneath it. Assignment and topic actions keep their position.

Participants, reply count and last activity scroll with the posts. Closed and event states are available in the State selector. Click the title, category controls, tags, assignment, wrench or share button to try them.

Content & platform notes

Based on TopicInboxHeader, TopicHeaderTags, AssignPlugin, DiscourseEventsPlugin and the existing topic actions. Assignment is intentionally anchored in the toolbar at both scroll positions. The same category and tag controls remain mounted while scrolling.

On macOS, notifications and profile belong to the window title bar. On narrower panes, tag overflow keeps every tag accessible. All edits and actions affect this mockup only.
